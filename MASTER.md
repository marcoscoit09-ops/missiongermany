# UI/UX Pro Max Design System: Apostolic Assembly Germany

## Concept
Premium, spacious, and inviting church website design inspired by the Hillsong reference. The aesthetic is clean, high-contrast, and focused on typography and subtle animations.

## 1. Color Palette
- **Primary Backgrounds:** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`), Off-White/Light Grey (`#F2F2F2`) for section separators.
- **Dark Sections:** Deep Charcoal/Black (`#1F1F1F`) for high-impact footer or special sections.
- **Typography:**
  - Headings: Solid Black (`#000000`)
  - Body Text: Dark Grey (`#464646`)
  - Metadata: Medium Grey (`#808080`)
- **Accents:**
  - Gold (`#C39D65`)
  - Sky Blue (`#289FD8`)

## 2. Typography
- **Primary Font Family:** "Proxima Nova", "Inter", "Outfit", or "Helvetica Neue", sans-serif.
- **Hierarchy:**
  - **H1, H2:** Bold or Semi-Bold, large size, tight letter spacing.
  - **Subheadings:** Light or Regular, increased letter-spacing.
  - **Body Text:** Regular weight, often centered for balance.

## 3. Micro-Animations
- **Scroll Reveals:** Soft fade-ins and slide-ups on scroll.
- **Hover Effects:** Buttons transition (border to solid, opacity change). Image cards scale up slightly on hover.
- **Transitions:** `0.3s ease` on all interactive elements.

## 4. Layout Style
- **Generous Whitespace:** Large padding/margins (e.g., `py-20` equivalents).
- **Hero:** Full bleed (ideally video or high-quality image) with high-contrast centered typography.
- **Navigation:** Clean, centered primary nav with a possible secondary top bar.
- **Cards:** Clean grid layouts with centered text below imagery.
